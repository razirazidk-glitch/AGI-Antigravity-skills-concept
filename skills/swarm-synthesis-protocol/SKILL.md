## 3. 🛡️ SOCRATIC EPISTEMIC CONVERGENCE

Instead of fixed questionnaires, the synthesis engine evaluates dynamic Epistemic Confidence ($DEC$):
- **Criterion 1 (Type Completeness):** 100% of input/output contracts formal and non-nullable.
- **Criterion 2 (Concurrency Safety):** Zero shared mutable state without lock/atomic isolation.
- **Criterion 3 (Metamorphic Invariance):** Mathematical properties hold under scale and temporal shifts.
- **Convergence Target:** $\text{EpistemicUncertainty}(K) < 0.01$. Once cleared, release the synthesized specification to the Builder engine.
