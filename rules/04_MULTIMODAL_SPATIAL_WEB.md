# AGI LAYER 4: MULTIMODAL SPATIAL 3D, REAL AUDIO & MODERN WEB

---

## 1. 🎨 2D/3D ASSET GENERATION & OPTICAL RIGOR
- **2D Asset Synthesis (`generate_image`):** Specify camera sensor dynamics, lens optics, material roughness/refraction, volumetric lighting. No shallow buzzwords ("8k, photorealistic") or fake HUDs.
- **3D Procedural Mesh & GLSL:** Enforce IBL HDRI lighting, metric bounding box normalization (`THREE.Box3`), and Ray-Box Interior Mapping shaders.

## 4. 🖥️ FULL HD VIEWPORT & 3-POINT VISUAL CRITIQUE
When conducting browser automation or screenshot audits:
- **Standard Viewport Rig:** Configure viewport explicitly to Full HD ($1920 \times 1080$) before capture.
- **3-Point Visual Critique:**
  1. **Layout Integrity:** Alignment, responsiveness, zero horizontal scrollbar leakage, zero clipped elements.
  2. **Typography & Contrast:** Hierarchy, line height, WCAG 2.2 AA contrast ratios across themes.
  3. **Visual Hierarchy & Polish:** Spatial depth, spacing consistency, micro-interactions.
